# UX changes

Live prototype: <https://vaesposito.github.io/TOME-updatedUX/> — the real
screens on invented data, nothing to sign in to.

TOME used to open onto a grid of project cards, and a space used to open onto a
feed of what had just changed. The redesign makes both answer the question
people actually arrive with. The portfolio now reads four ways — a catalogue by
goal, a catalogue by function, one overview of everything, or the week in prose
— and every space has a Home saying what it is, who keeps it, where its
material comes from and what needs attention. Around that, the wiki became
editable where it is read, notifications became something to choose rather than
a bell to ignore, and five guided tours walk a newcomer through the lot.

## The catalogue and its two axes

- TOME is a section in the main navigation, listing where it can take you instead of opening onto one page.
- The catalogue reads two ways: by goal and area, or by the function that owns the space.
- Both readings share the same cards, counts, search and filters, so only the vocabulary changes.
- An Overview reads every space at once, and its rows open in a new tab so the survey stays put.

## Creating a space

- A three-step form — name it, connect its sources, invite people — replaces the seven-step wizard.
- Where the space goes is one question, asked on the axis it belongs to, one field per row.
- The data steward is chosen on the invite step, not inferred from whoever was invited first.
- Ingest runs weekly by default, and can be set to hourly.
- A new space starts with one page written by the agent rather than empty placeholders.

## A space's Home

- Replaces the activity feed: an ask box, the recap, what needs attention, what you were tagged in, the team, the sources, milestones and KPIs.
- A question typed in the ask box is carried into the agent's chat and sent.
- Cards can be dragged, hidden and brought back, and the arrangement follows you into every space.
- Empty looks the same on every card, and always offers the one thing that would fill it.
- A goal or area gets a home about what is below it: its projects, their pooled sources and people.

## A space's Overview

- Changed this week, folding events that belong together into one line per change.
- Open decisions, with anything already settled dropped.
- The description in full, and when the space was last read and is read next.

## The space navigation and its settings

- An opt-in rail of seven destinations replaces a column that was also trying to be a table of contents.
- The tree reads as a tree: folder marks, visible indents, folders closed until opened, and a count on every row.
- Sources are grouped by the system they came from, and a system never connected says so.
- Meetings replaces Standup: each series a space listens to, and what came of each call.
- The header offers Run ingest, says which pages a run rewrites, and carries Following beside it.

## The wiki and its editors

- Edit turns the tree into something to tidy: rows drag or step up and down from the keyboard, and delete where they sit.
- A page can also be deleted inside its own editor, which leaves you in the folder that held it.
- The editor's toolbar is down to three treatments: status as plain text, one select style for the page's three choices, and Delete, Cancel and Save at the end.
- A page is named and renamed where it is written; no filenames reach a reader.
- New pages, folders, import and export are reachable from the wiki itself, not only from settings.
- The header tells a person's edit from the agent's, and a page the next ingest may overwrite says so.
- The wiki has a glossary of its own, holding the only search box left in TOME.
- A page, or one passage of it, can be tagged into other spaces and shows up in their content and their agent's answers.

## Notifications and following

- An inbox of everything received, narrowed by status, type, space or origin.
- A Watching tree over the whole portfolio, so a reader chooses what reaches them.
- Each row can arrive in a chat space or a direct message, as it happens or as a daily or weekly digest.
- One word for one idea: Watch and Follow are both Following, offering Everything, Important, Mentions or Off.
- Quiet hours, and preferences set per space as well as overall.
- Saving a wiki edit asks who should hear about it.

## The guided tours

- A tour button on the catalogue asks which of five walkthroughs you want, each saying what it covers.
- The spotlight lands on the control the step names, opening whatever hides it and putting it back afterwards.
- Steps explaining an idea rather than a control appear at once, in the middle of the window.
- A tour borrows a space that really has pages, so a newcomer is not told there is nothing to show.

## The agent and chat delivery

- Where no agent is deployed, the corner holds a stand-in with the real chrome that says nothing is being answered.
- The panel stays open, on the same tab and size, as you move around TOME.
- A note beside its header gives the three steps for reaching the agent from chat.
- The agent is told about pages other spaces tagged in, attributed to where they came from.

## The platform shell

- The welcome band greets you by name, its tabs moved onto the page below and its buttons at one size.
- An information mark beside the title gives the release and build date, in a note you can copy from.
